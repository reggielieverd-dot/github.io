Vantaris

A next-generation 3D printed 8.3m multi-mission unmanned maritime system (USV), with advantages being speed of deployment, modular payloads and interoperability.

Funds have been committed from a family office, specialised in defence. Another sum is already secured to allow a quick start, the engine is in order, naval architecture is in finalisation.

The vessel has already been selected by the Portuguese Navy for a multi-phase co-development programme and production has been relocated to another NATO country, the Netherlands, production of the first vessel underway as we speak, and will be finished before the end of June.

The platform will be demonstrated at REPMUS 2026, NATO's largest unmanned systems exercise, providing exposure to procurement teams from over 20 NATO nations.

The Netherlands has a demonstrable and reliable track record in boat production and for quality production it is the country with perhaps the largest quantity of good boat builders. As a result of this strategical decision to secure reliable delivery in the Netherlands, a further 350k is initially sought, ahead of Series A to scale the business.
